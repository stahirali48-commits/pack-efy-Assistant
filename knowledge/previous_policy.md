Superseded By: Version 2.0, effective 1 October 2026

## Previous Rules

This archived policy records rules used before 1 October 2026. Use it only for historical questions, never for current policy advice.

Karachi delivery required 3 to 5 working days after dispatch. Other-city delivery required 6 to 8 working days.

Delivery charges were PKR 250 within Karachi and PKR 400 outside Karachi. Free standard delivery within Karachi applied to orders above PKR 20,000.

Customers could request a return within 3 calendar days of receiving an unused product in its original packaging. Damaged or incorrect products had to be reported within 12 hours, with the order number and photographs.

## Changes in Version 2.0

The current policy increased the return window from 3 to 7 calendar days and the damage-reporting window from 12 to 24 hours.

Karachi delivery changed from 3–5 to 2–3 working days. Other-city delivery changed from 6–8 to 4–6 working days.

The Karachi free-delivery threshold changed from above PKR 20,000 to above PKR 15,000.
