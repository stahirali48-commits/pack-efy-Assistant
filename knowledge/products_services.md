## 3. Custom Printing Service

PACKEFY Packaging offers custom printing on selected packaging products.

Customers may request business logos, brand names, product information, and simple artwork.

The minimum custom printing order is 500 units per design.

Artwork must be approved by the customer before production begins. Production time starts after artwork approval.

Custom printing availability depends on the selected material and product type.

## 4. Bulk Order Service

Customers ordering larger quantities may request a customized quotation.

The sales team reviews product type, dimensions, material, printing requirements, and quantity before preparing a quotation.

A quotation is not considered a confirmed order until the customer approves it and completes the required payment procedure.

## 5. Product Selection Guidance

Customers should select packaging based on product weight, storage conditions, presentation requirements, and transportation needs.

Coffee and moisture-sensitive products may require barrier packaging. Bakery products may use paper bags or window pouches. Online sellers may prefer corrugated boxes for shipping.

Employees should not claim that every packaging material is suitable for every food product. Material suitability must be confirmed from the relevant product specification.

## 6. Product Information Limitations

The prices shown above are educational sample prices only. They are not guaranteed selling prices.

The final quotation may depend on quantity, custom printing, material, and size.

Some product features may vary between sizes. Employees must consult the relevant product details instead of assuming that every size has identical features.
