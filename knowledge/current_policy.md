# PACKEFY Packaging — Current Policy

Document ID: DOC-003
Policy Version: 2.0
Effective Date: 1 October 2026
Document Status: Current
Currency: PKR
Time Zone: Pakistan Standard Time (PKT)

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the current delivery, payment, cancellation, return, and refund policies of PACKEFY Packaging. Employees must use this version when answering questions about current company rules.

## 2. Order Processing

Orders confirmed before 2:00 PM on a working day are normally processed on the same working day.

Orders confirmed after 2:00 PM are processed on the next working day.

The company operates Monday to Saturday. Sunday is a non-working day.

An order is considered confirmed only after payment verification and order confirmation by the sales team.

## 3. Delivery Policy

Karachi deliveries normally require 2 to 3 working days after dispatch.

Deliveries to other cities in Pakistan normally require 4 to 6 working days after dispatch.

Standard delivery charges within Karachi are PKR 350.

Deliveries outside Karachi have standard shipping charges of PKR 500.

Orders above PKR 15,000 qualify for free standard delivery within Karachi.

Free delivery does not apply to locations outside Karachi, urgent shipping, or special transportation arrangements.

Delivery estimates may change because of weather conditions, transport disruptions, or unexpected operational issues.

## 4. Return Policy

Customers may request a return within 7 calendar days of receiving a standard, unused, and unopened product.

Products must remain in their original packaging and must not show signs of use.

Customers must provide their order number and a clear explanation of the return request.

The support team reviews every request before approving a return.

## 5. Damaged or Incorrect Products

Customers must report damaged, missing, or incorrect products within 24 hours of delivery.

Customers should provide photographs of the packaging, received products, and visible damage.

After verification, the company may approve a replacement or refund.

## 6. Special Exceptions

Exception 1 — Custom Printed Products:

Custom printed products cannot normally be returned because they are produced according to customer-approved artwork. However, a verified manufacturing defect may qualify for replacement if reported within 48 hours of delivery.

Exception 2 — Free Delivery Eligibility:

The free delivery threshold of PKR 15,000 applies only to standard deliveries within Karachi. Orders delivered outside Karachi must pay the applicable shipping charges, even when their order value exceeds PKR 15,000.

## 7. Cancellation Policy

Customers may request cancellation before production or dispatch begins.

Orders already in production or dispatched cannot normally be cancelled.

Custom printed orders cannot be cancelled after the customer approves the final artwork.

## 8. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 5 to 7 working days.

The refund is made through the original payment method wherever possible.

The customer receives a confirmation after the refund has been processed.

## 9. Employee Responsibilities

Employees must follow this current policy when answering customer questions.

Employees must not use archived policy rules for current situations.

If a situation is not covered by this document, employees must refer it to the support supervisor instead of inventing a rule.
