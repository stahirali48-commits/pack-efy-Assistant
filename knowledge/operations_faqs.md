## Quotation Procedure

Record product, size, quantity, material, printing needs, delivery city, and contact details. Use DOC-002 for specifications; sales confirms final prices.

## Order and Complaint Handling

Verify payment before confirming an order. Operations prepares orders for dispatch. Use DOC-003 for current rules, never archived DOC-004.

For damaged or incorrect items, request the order number, delivery date, and photographs. Check DOC-003 deadlines; refer unclear cases to the supervisor.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can customers request custom logos?
A: Yes, on selected products, subject to product suitability and minimum quantity rules in DOC-002.

Q: Are sample prices guaranteed?
A: No. They are fictional examples. The sales team confirms final prices.

Q: Which delivery rules apply now?
A: DOC-003, Version 2.0, Current.

Q: When is the archived policy used?
A: Only for historical questions. Refer to DOC-004, Version 1.0, Archived.

Q: What if information is missing?
A: Say, "I could not find enough information in the provided documents." Do not guess; refer decisions to the supervisor.
